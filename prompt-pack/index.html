Three samples, free

Rain Confession (Veo 3). Medium close-up, a woman in a soaked trench coat stands under a flickering streetlamp at night, rain streaking through the light, she looks up and whispers one short line. Slow push-in. Wet pavement reflections, teal-and-amber grade. Tip: keep the spoken line under 8 words — dialogue truncates past that.

Perfume Reveal (Veo 3). Macro shot, a glass perfume bottle on black stone, a ribbon of mist curls around it as a beam of light sweeps across the label. Slow dolly-in, luxury commercial look. Tip: name the label text exactly, or ask for a blank label and add text in post.

Whale Pass (Veo 3 / Sora 2). Underwater wide shot, a freediver holds still as a humpback whale glides past, sun rays cutting blue water. Serene, majestic, documentary grade. Tip: stillness of the human subject is what sells the scale.
