Quick Start: First Prompt in 30 Seconds

No account, no install, nothing to upload. Open the generator, answer a few fields, copy the result.

Steps

  1. Open the AI Video Prompt Generator.
  2. Pick the target model: Veo 3 / 3.1, Sora 2, or Any.
  3. Optionally tap a template (Cinematic, Product Ad, Social Short, Documentary, 3D Animation, Anime).
  4. Fill Subject and Action first; add Setting, shot size, camera movement and lighting only if you know them. Empty fields are skipped.
  5. Copy the prompt — or, on the Veo page, copy the JSON version — and paste it into your video model.
  6. After a bad take, change one field and generate again.
